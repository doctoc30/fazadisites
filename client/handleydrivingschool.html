At Handley Driving School, our mission is to provide you with the best driving education possible. A proudly growing driving school with 16 cars on the road. We meticulously select only the finest instructors to join the HDS family, ensuring that our students receive the highest quality education. We know that learning to drive can be a daunting task, which is why we're here to help you every step of the way.

Founded by Joseph Handley, a Grade A Approved Driving Instructor, who has been delivering top-notch instruction since March 2022. With over 20 years of extensive motoring experience, including his background as an HGV driver – lorry-mounted cranes and car transporters, as well as being a skilled motorcyclist, he confidently supports our instructors at every turn. His wealth of knowledge guarantees that our learners receive the finest guidance on the road, setting them up for success.

We offer driving tuition for all levels of experience, PASSPLUS, refresher courses and for those looking for a new career ADI training. Whatever you need we'll make sure you have the knowledge and experience necessary to become a safe and confident driver. Choose Handley Driving School, where we pride ourselves on our high pass rate and most importantly our 100% honest, upfront and reliable service.
