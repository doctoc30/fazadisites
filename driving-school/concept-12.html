FAQ

How much are lessons?
Lessons start from £35/hour. Block bookings and packages offer great discounts — see our packages above.
How long is each lesson?
Standard lessons are 1 hour, but we also offer 2-hour and 3-hour sessions for intensive learning.
Do you offer test preparation?
Yes! Our Test Prep Package includes a mock test, car hire for your practical test, and a warm-up lesson.
Can I book weekend lessons?
Absolutely — we offer weekend and evening lessons at no extra charge, subject to availability.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfer. All online payments are secure.
